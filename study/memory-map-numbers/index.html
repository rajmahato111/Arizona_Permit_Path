How this guide works & key numbers

The animated study guide’s opener: how each flashcard works, plus the number strip worth memorizing first.

How the guide studies

Each flashcard gives a question, the answer, a one-line reason, and a short memory hook. The PDF repeats items on purpose—repetition is part of the drill.

Study trick from the guideRead the question, cover the answer, say your answer out loud, then check. Repeat any card you miss twice.

This app turns those cards into short lessons and multiple-choice practice. Items are study-guide practice aids—not official MVD exam questions.
